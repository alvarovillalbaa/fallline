A FEW TRAIL NOTES

Find a rhythm.
Then find your line.

01 /

Carve, don’t wrestle.

Smooth turns leave you room to react. Tuck for speed, brake before a tight passage, and steer around the trees.

02 /

A little air goes a long way.

Tap Space or Hop to jump. Low rocks can be cleared; trees need a different line. Ramps give you an extra lift.

03 /

Keep it clean.

Pass between the flags. Each miss adds four seconds. Your best completed adjusted time is the one to beat.

On touch screens, hold the arrow, brake or tuck buttons and tap Hop. Release a held control to return to a neutral stance. Holding Hop does not make the board jump repeatedly.

Your adjusted time is the run time plus four seconds per missed flag. Only completed course runs can replace a personal best; free rides and crashes do not. Records stay in this browser when storage is available.

This is an arcade game, not sports instruction. Visual motion is essential to play; Stable camera reduces camera movement. Sound is optional and starts only after you enable it.
